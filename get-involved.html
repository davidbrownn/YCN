Get involved

Three paths, one mission.

Every part of this work needs someone different. Find the one that sounds like you — then send one email.

  • A

    High school volunteers

    You sign up through your state chapter, complete our training, and then lead workshops in real classrooms. No prior experience needed — most volunteers have never taught before their first session.

    • Training covers the curriculum, classroom management, and facilitation
    • Each lesson you write for us counts as volunteer hours
    • Chapters meet weekly; you teach as often as your schedule allows
    Start volunteering
  • B

    Educators and hosts

    Bring YCN into your classroom or after-school program. We provide the materials, the training, and the volunteers. If there's no chapter near you, the entire curriculum is free to download and teach yourself.

    • Built for grades 3–5, roughly 35 minutes per lesson
    • Nonpartisan by policy — no political parties in any lecture
    • Homeschool co-ops and libraries are welcome, not an exception
    Partner with us Browse the curriculum
  • C

    Funders and partners

    Your grant or sponsorship funds curriculum development and chapter expansion. We are pursuing 501(c)(3) status; support today goes directly into materials and getting new state chapters teaching.

    • 12 active state chapters, expanding
    • Open curriculum — everything we build is public and free
    • We report reach honestly, including the numbers we can't verify
    Fund our mission

Not sure which one you are?

Email us anyway and say what you're hoping to do. We answer every message.
